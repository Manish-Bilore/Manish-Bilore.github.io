InSAR time series for urban land subsidence

InSAR
Sentinel-1
subsidence
XGBoost
thesis

M.Tech thesis. Two open-source Sentinel-1 time-series chains, MintPy on the Alaska Satellite Facility cloud and LiCSBAS on LiCSAR interferograms, run over the same frame to separate what a subsidence map measures from what its processing decides. Lucknow reaches −63.9 mm/yr; the deformation field then trains an XGBoost susceptibility model (AUC 0.705) for the ground with no scatterers to measure.

CFD of airflow and thermal conditions for experimental solar housing in Tanzania, validated against measured PET and UTCI with IoT sensor units built in Copenhagen and monitored remotely.
